# Timecone.ai & ChronoCone Project Overview

## Overview

**Timecone.ai** is a visionary planning system moving beyond "flat time" to manage multiple possible futures simultaneously. It is designed to act as an "Institutional Brain" that overcomes the Consensus Trap (forced, inaccurate consensus) by allowing organizations to explore discordance, alternative scenarios, and systemic "Idea Darwinism."

At the heart of Timecone.ai sits **ChronoCone**, the deterministic calculation engine powering the system. ChronoCone solves complex planning problems across different domains by providing a flexible framework that supports automated planning, human-in-the-loop planning, and collaborative planning across different organizational levels.

The "cone" represents widening fields of possible future states. Plans expand either through refinement (general plans developing into specific ones) or temporal extension (short-term plans extending into longer horizons). 

## Purpose

Timecone.ai uses the ChronoCone engine to support macro-level probabilistic planning by managing discrete plan templates and instances. Planning organizations create and maintain templates that specify what data the plan requires, what operations to perform on that data, and what results to produce. 

Each instantiated plan is a strict, rule-based Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)—a deterministic point-in-time calculation. By generating arrays of these deterministic DAGs (representing conflicting "Permissionless Assertion Layers" or alternative futures), Timecone.ai allows agentic AI to reason at a high level. Agents can propose alternative futures, and the ChronoCone engine calculates them deterministically, grounding hallucination-prone AI in verifiable math.

## Key Capabilities

- **Institutional Brain Ontology**: High-level mapping of business metrics, preventing siloed logic.
- **Plan Lifecycle Management**: Creation, versioning, and execution of deterministic DAG plan templates.
- **Probabilistic Aggregation**: Assembling probability distributions by aggregating multiple, potentially contradictory base plans.
- **Decoupled Execution Model**: The calculation engine routes computation (transformers/shifters/filters) to distributed blob storage, decoupling logic from execution data.
- **Agentic Governance**: Human-initiated MCP workflows (Planners, Analysts, SIs) guiding AI agents to construct robust plans on a mathematically sound substrate.

## Target Domains

Timecone.ai serves diverse planning domains including:

- **Logistics**: Warehouse labor planning, transportation scheduling, capacity management
- **Manufacturing**: Production planning, resource allocation, supply chain coordination
- **Healthcare**: Staff scheduling, resource planning, patient flow management
- And other domains requiring probabilistic planning and structured time-series data management
