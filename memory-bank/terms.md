# Terms

**Prediction Market**
: A market where participants trade contracts whose payoffs are tied to the outcome of future events. Examples: Polymarket, Kalshi.

**Arbitrage**
: Simultaneously buying and selling equivalent assets in different markets to profit from price differences. In prediction markets, this means taking opposite sides of the same or related event on different platforms to lock in a risk-free profit when prices diverge.

**Binary Outcome Market**
: A prediction market where the contract pays a fixed amount (typically $1) if an event occurs and $0 if it does not.

**PolyMarket**
: A decentralized prediction market platform built on the Polygon blockchain, offering binary outcome markets on a wide range of topics.

**Kalshi**
: A CFTC-regulated prediction market exchange in the United States, offering binary outcome markets on a range of events.

**Spread**
: In this context, the difference in price for equivalent positions across markets. A spread greater than zero plus transaction costs represents an arbitrage opportunity.

**Market Maker**
: An entity that provides liquidity by quoting both buy and sell prices for a market, profiting from the bid-ask spread.

**Liquidity**
: The degree to which a market can absorb trades without significantly moving the price. Low liquidity can make arbitrage opportunities unexploitable in practice.

**Slippage**
: The difference between the expected price of a trade and the price at which the trade is actually executed, often caused by low liquidity or high volatility.
